# X-Atlas/Orion: research-use license memo

Date: 2026-10-07. Scope: **non-commercial academic research use in the research track only** (experiment
N3). This memo does **not** change the competition status. X-Atlas remains `BLOCKED_PENDING_PERMISSION` for
any prize-bearing Virtual Cell Challenge entry (`reports/competition_v2/xatlas_permission_status.md`,
`virtual_cell.competition_v2.licensing`). Nothing derived from X-Atlas under this memo may enter a competition
candidate.

## 4. Permitted use under this memo

**Permitted:** non-commercial academic research (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 §1(i), "NonCommercial": not primarily
intended for or directed towards commercial advantage or monetary compensation). N3 is a preregistered
scientific analysis that:

* produces no product, no competition entry and no paid service;
* reads X-Atlas only in research-track code (`scripts/research_v3/`, `src/virtual_cell/analysis/`). Competition
  code paths remain gated by `licensing.STATUS` (`HCT116`, `HEK293T` = `BLOCKED_PENDING_PERMISSION`), which is
  **unchanged**.

**Not permitted under this memo:**

* any use in a prize-bearing VCC 2026 submission, including statistics, weights, selected hyperparameters or
  model choices derived from X-Atlas;
* any commercial use;
* relicensing.

**Judgement call, stated openly.** The NC clause turns on the purpose of the use. Academic publication of
findings is ordinarily non-commercial. If this project's output were ever tied to a commercial or prize
purpose, this memo would not cover it and Xaira's written permission would be required.

## 5. Citation and attribution requirements

Any report, figure, preprint or paper that uses N3 results must:

1. cite Huang et al. 2025 (bioRxiv doi:10.1101/2025.06.11.659105) and name Xaira Therapeutics as the
   originator;
2. state the license (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) with a link, and indicate if changes were made. We derive pseudobulk
   statistics, which are adapted material;
3. credit the SLAF re-release (`slaf-project/X-Atlas-Orion`, revision `598aa544…`) as the copy used.

`reports/n3_results.md` carries this attribution block.

## 7. Redistribution restrictions

* **No redistribution of the raw data or of row-level derived matrices** (per-perturbation pseudobulk,
  split-part means) from this repository. Derived arrays stay in git-ignored `outputs/` and `data/processed/`.
* **ShareAlike:** if adapted material (e.g. pseudobulk matrices) were ever shared, it must be under CC BY-NC-SA
  4.0 (or a BY-NC-SA-compatible license), with attribution and a change notice.
* Aggregate scientific results are reported under the attribution requirements above: metrics, curves and
  small summary tables per context, with no reconstruction of per-perturbation expression profiles.
* Figure-source tables committed under `data/figure_sources/` must contain only aggregate statistics for the
  X-Atlas contexts.
